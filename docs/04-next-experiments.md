# Proposed next experiments

Priority: vary **physical mechanisms**, not merely push the existing median toward unity.

1. **Parametric wave forcing:** replace independent OU forcing by a global periodically modulated stiffness/tension term (Faraday-like), with independent thermal noise retained.
2. **Impact synchronization:** couple rebound timing/phase to the local wave elevation and velocity; retain local slope feedback to the particle.
3. **Cross-mode energy transfer:** add weak physically specified nonlinear string tension; compare with the original linear-mode dynamics at equal thermal control.
4. **Separate tests:** require finite T=0 modal energy, shape comparison to the complete Planck mean, and R_n≈1 **for the same low modes** across several T, not just a low all-mode median.
5. **Convergence:** increase time and independent seeds, quantify autocorrelation times, convergence in spectral cutoff and fixed-string physical parameters.
6. **Null experiments:** deactivate one coupling at a time to identify mechanisms rather than merely fitting parameters.

Do not infer emergence of quantum statistics from an exponential classical modal-energy distribution or a fitted Planck-like curve.
